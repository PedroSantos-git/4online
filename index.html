Pagamentos segurosSecure payments

Os pagamentos dos serviços são processados pelo Stripe, o comerciante registado (Merchant of Record), através do serviço Link. O Stripe emite a fatura e trata do IVA. No extrato do cartão aparece «LINK.COM*».Payments for the services are processed by Stripe, the Merchant of Record, through its Link service. Stripe issues the invoice and handles VAT. Your card statement shows "LINK.COM*".

Cancelar e reembolsosCancelling and refunds
